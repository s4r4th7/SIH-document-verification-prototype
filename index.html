Supported documents

  • 01 Aadhaar Card 12‑digit UID, name, DOB, address
  • 02 PAN Card 10‑character permanent account number
  • 03 Passport MRZ lines, passport number, expiry
  • 04 Visa visa type, validity window, sponsor
  • 05 Income Certificate issuing authority, income bracket, date

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Add a photo or scan of the document. JPG, PNG and PDF are accepted.

  2. 2

    Extract

    DSentinel reads the text on the page and pulls out the fields specific to that document type.

  3. 3

    Verify

    Each field is checked against the document's own format rules, and anything that doesn't match is flagged.
